# Smart Pocket Bookmarks

AI-powered bookmarks that turn saved links into a knowledge library.

## 中文介绍

**AI智能书签，让收藏进化为知识宝库。**

收藏越来越多，却越来越难找？Pocket Bookmarks 不只是保存链接，而是用 AI 帮你整理、分类、检索和分享收藏内容，把零散网页沉淀成真正可复用的个人知识库。

### 核心功能

- **一键保存，多端访问**：快速收藏网页，随时随地查看和管理重要链接。
- **AI 智能整理**：自动推荐分类与标签，减少手动归档成本。
- **分类 + 标签管理**：用一级分类和多标签组织资料，让收藏更清晰。
- **快速搜索与筛选**：按关键词、分类、标签快速定位内容，告别“收藏了但找不到”。
- **常用 Top 10**：自动展示高频访问书签，把最常用资源放到最顺手的位置。
- **访问统计与可视化**：通过数据了解收藏使用情况，发现真正高价值的资源。
- **多种展示与排序**：支持按访问次数、创建时间、分类、标签等方式查看书签。
- **全局分享与分类分享**：可分享全部收藏，也可只分享指定分类，适合团队协作和知识合集发布。

### 为什么选择 Pocket Bookmarks？

它解决的不是“如何存更多链接”，而是“如何更快找到、理解并复用收藏”。从资料收集、日常浏览到团队分享，Pocket Bookmarks 帮你把浏览器收藏夹升级为高效、清晰、可分享的知识宝库。

## English Introduction

**AI-powered bookmarks that turn saved links into a knowledge library.**

Too many bookmarks, but never the one you need? Pocket Bookmarks goes beyond saving links. It uses AI to help you organize, categorize, search, and share your bookmarks, turning scattered web pages into a reusable personal knowledge base.

### Key Features

- **One-click saving, access anywhere**: Save web pages quickly and manage important links across devices.
- **AI-powered organization**: Get smart category and tag suggestions to reduce manual sorting.
- **Categories + tags**: Structure your resources with primary categories and multiple tags.
- **Fast search and filtering**: Find bookmarks by keyword, category, or tag without digging through folders.
- **Top 10 frequently used bookmarks**: Keep your most-used resources within instant reach.
- **Bookmark stats and visualization**: Understand usage patterns and identify your most valuable resources.
- **Flexible views and sorting**: Browse by visit count, creation date, category, tag, and more.
- **Global and category-based sharing**: Share your full collection or selected categories with friends, coworkers, or teams.

### Why Pocket Bookmarks?

Pocket Bookmarks is built for people who save useful links but need a smarter way to retrieve and reuse them. It helps turn everyday browsing, research, and team sharing into an organized knowledge workflow.
